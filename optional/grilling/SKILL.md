---
name: grilling
description: Clarify an unclear goal, requirements, or plan through focused questions. Use when the user asks to grill an idea, decide what to build, or resolve product tradeoffs before implementation.
---

When the goal is unclear, first establish the desired outcome and constraints. Interview the user about the plan until you reach a shared understanding. Walk each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.

Ask one question at a time, provide your recommended answer, and wait for feedback before continuing.

Look up facts in the repository or environment rather than asking the user for information you can inspect. Leave product choices and preferences to the user.

Summarize the agreed goal, scope, constraints, and remaining decisions. Do not implement the plan until the user confirms the shared understanding.

Adapted from the locally installed `grilling` skill and [Matt Pocock's Skills repository](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/grilling).
